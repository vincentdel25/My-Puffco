Metric definitions & percentile method
  • Average hit: mean duration of completed timers.
  • Median hit: middle completed duration.
  • Top-half duration: average of the longest half of completed timers, rounded up for odd samples.
  • Points per timer: rank points divided by all timers, including interrupted timers with zero points.
  • Consistency: 100 × (1 − min(standard deviation ÷ mean duration, 1)). Needs at least two completed timers.
  • Completion rate: percent of timers that finished manually instead of being interrupted.
  • Repeatability: percent of completed durations within ±20% of their median (minimum tolerance 0.1 seconds).
  • Recent form: average completed duration in the last five timers minus the preceding five. Requires a full 10-timer window and completed entries in both halves.

Current metrics use the latest 10 saved timers. Reference values come from all earlier 10-timer windows, excluding those latest 10 timers. At least 30 saved timers and 10 valid reference values are required.

Percentile = 100 × (reference values below yours + half the ties) ÷ valid reference values, rounded and bounded to 1st–99th. Equal values receive the middle rank. Each metric is compared separately; no composite percentile is invented.

Higher means a larger metric value relative to your history. Timings are manual and saved only in this browser.
