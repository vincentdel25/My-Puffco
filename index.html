Setup & troubleshooting
  1. Replace the old index.html in your existing GitHub repository with this file and commit it.
  2. Open your existing GitHub Pages website in Bluefy. Confirm the title says Phase 2A. Refresh if it still shows Phase 1.
  3. Close other Puffco controllers, wake your Peak Pro, and tap Connect Peak Pro. Choose your device.
  4. Keep Bluefy in the foreground until the scan finishes. Tap Copy log when it says Scan complete.

The picker shows all nearby devices so a changed device name will not hide your Peak Pro. Choose your own Peak Pro. The app requests the two custom service UUIDs from your screenshots, plus standard battery and device information services. Other services may exist but remain inaccessible to this page.

If a read requires authentication, its error is logged and the scan continues. A timeout disconnects the device; reconnect to try again. Logs include the Bluetooth device name and raw values, which may contain identifiers. No logs are sent to a server by this page.
